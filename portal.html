Australian Laws — Regulatory Reference
  • AML/CTF
    Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (Cth)
    Primary legislation governing reporting entities, suspicious matter reporting, and AUSTRAC's supervisory powers over digital currency exchange providers.
    Penalty: Up to life imprisonment / A$660,000
  • CCA
    Criminal Code Act 1995 (Cth) — Part 10.2
    Covers money laundering offences including dealing with proceeds of crime, structuring transactions to avoid reporting, and financing terrorism.
    Penalty: Up to 25 years imprisonment
  • DCER
    AUSTRAC Digital Currency Exchange Provider Rules 2018
    Regulations specific to DCE providers including registration requirements, transaction monitoring, and customer due diligence obligations.
    Penalty: A$3.3 million per offence (corporate)
  • PA
    Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)
    Governs collection, use and disclosure of personal information. Compliance officers must handle personal data in accordance with Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).
    Penalty: Up to A$2.5 million (serious breach)
  • CS Act
    Crimes Act 1914 (Cth) — Section 3ZQU
    Covers obstruction of Commonwealth officers and failure to comply with notices issued under AML/CTF legislation.
    Penalty: Up to 2 years imprisonment
  • ASIC
    Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001
    ASIC's powers in relation to financial services misconduct, fraudulent digital asset schemes, and unregistered managed investment schemes.
    Penalty: Up to 15 years imprisonment
